The data for this project was obtained from NYC TLC Trip Data.

We used the Green Trips Taxi data and Taxi Zone Lookup files.

We created the following charts:

Top 5 pickup Zones by Demand:
    We observed that North Harlem pickup zone has the most demand overall in a month.

Hourly Demand in a Day:
    We observed that evenings have the highest demand overall in a day.

Top 3 Pickup Zones with most revenue:
     we observed that the pickup zone with most demand was not the with most revenue.
